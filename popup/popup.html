Clearing history does not bring anything back — the extension just stops remembering those records. The data file moves history and lists to another browser.

How it works
  1. Turn on the modules you need — they will appear on open X tabs by themselves.
  2. Open the X page you want to clean: timeline, Following or messages.
  3. A panel appears in the bottom right corner: start, progress, Stop and the log live there.

Data stays in your browser. The protect list and action history are kept in the extension storage, and requests go only to x.com. There are no servers, tokens or analytics.

Mass actions hit X limits: 50–100 unfollows per run is fine, thousands a day means a temporary block. Everything deleted is permanent.
