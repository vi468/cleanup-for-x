Privacy policy

Applies to version 1.13.0 and newer. Русская версия

In short: Cleanup for X does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. The extension runs only in your browser and sends requests only to x.com (and twitter.com) — the site whose tab you opened yourself.

What is stored

Everything is stored locally, in your browser’s chrome.storage:

KeyWhat it is
tweets, reposts, quotes, likes, ghostlikes, dms, unfollowwhether a module is on (setting)
историяids of tweets, reposts, likes (including stuck ones), conversations and accounts already acted on — so the same action is not repeated
защитаthe “who to leave alone” list: handles and account ids
архив_списки, архив_репосты, архив_цитаты, архив_лайкиids from X archive files that you chose in the panel
задания, задания_очередь, queryidsbackground queue progress and working request identifiers

The extension also reads the ct0 CSRF cookie and the lists of your tweets, likes, follows and conversations that x.com itself shows on the page, so it can perform the action you picked. That data does not leave the browser and is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This data never leaves the device. You can delete it with the “clear” buttons in the popup or by removing the extension. The “export to file” file is created locally and saved where the browser is told to save it.

What is transmitted

Nothing — to third parties. Requests to x.com are made with your own cookies, as if you pressed buttons on the site. The extension has no servers and uses no analytics, trackers or ad networks.

Permissions and why

  • storage — keep settings, history and the protect list locally.
  • cookies — read the ct0 CSRF cookie so background actions (unfollow, reposts, quotes, likes, stuck likes) can run as you while the tab is closed. The cookie value is sent nowhere except x.com.
  • host_permissions (https://x.com/*, https://twitter.com/*) — insert buttons and panels on X pages and call the X API.

The extension does not request browser history, other sites, tabs with other addresses, or files on disk.

Children

The extension is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect data about them.

Changes

If the policy changes, the new version will be published together with a new version of the extension.
